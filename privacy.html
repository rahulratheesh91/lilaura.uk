Data protection

Privacy Notice.

How LilAura handles personal information when you use the website, WhatsApp Concierge or Etsy.

Who is the data controller?

LilAura UK acts as the controller for personal information submitted directly through website enquiries and styling services. Etsy separately processes personal data associated with purchases made through the Etsy marketplace.

What information do we collect?

  • Contact details and message content when you contact us.
  • Information you voluntarily share through WhatsApp Concierge.
  • Order information Etsy provides to us for fulfilment.
  • Local browser storage used for website features such as the shopping bag and wishlist.

How do we use it?

We use information to answer enquiries, fulfil orders, provide customer support, maintain records where legally required and operate the website.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law gives you rights including access, correction, erasure, restriction and objection. Contact shop@lilaura.uk for privacy requests.

Third parties

Relevant service providers may include Etsy, WhatsApp/Meta, delivery partners and any contact-form provider you enable in the website configuration.
