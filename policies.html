Returns & Refunds Policy

At LilAura, we want you to be completely satisfied with your ultra-luxury anti-tarnish jewellery. As all our transactions are securely processed through our official Etsy storefront (LilAuraElegance), our returns and refunds are governed by our Etsy shop policies and UK Distance Selling Regulations.

  • Returns Window: You have 14 days from the date of delivery to contact us via Etsy messages to request a return, and 30 days to dispatch the item back to us.
  • Condition: Items must be returned unworn, in their original condition, and with all original packaging and tags intact.
  • Exclusions: For hygiene reasons, pierced earrings cannot be returned or exchanged unless faulty. Custom or personalized items are also non-refundable.
  • Process: Once your return is received and inspected, a refund will automatically be applied to your original method of payment via the Etsy platform.

Treats Loyalty Scheme Terms & Conditions

Participation & Collection of Points

The LilAura "Treats" program rewards our returning customers. Currently, as our checkout operates via Etsy, loyalty rewards and discount codes are distributed manually to repeat buyers via email or direct Etsy messages following a qualifying purchase.

Exclusions

Points/rewards cannot be earned on shipping costs, gift wrap fees, or the purchase of gift cards. Wholesale or B2B orders are excluded from the consumer Treats scheme.

Treats Vouchers & Redemption

Vouchers issued by LilAura will be provided as unique Etsy Promo Codes. They must be entered at the Etsy checkout prior to payment completion. Vouchers hold no cash value and cannot be applied retroactively to previous orders.

Returns

If an item purchased using a Treats voucher is returned, the refund will equal the actual amount paid. The voucher will not be reissued.

LilAura reserves the right to offer surprise upgrades, early access to new collections, or free gifts to loyalty members at our sole discretion.

WEEE Regulations

The Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment (WEEE) Directive is UK legislation aimed at reducing the amount of electronic waste going to landfills. While LilAura primarily retails traditional and anti-tarnish metal jewellery, should you purchase any item from us containing electronic components (e.g., LED gift boxes or quartz watches), you can return your old equivalent item to us for safe disposal under the WEEE take-back scheme. Please contact our Customer Care team for a return address.

Corporate Responsibility

At LilAura, we believe that elegance should not come at an ethical cost. We are committed to:

  • Sustainable Durability: By utilizing advanced anti-tarnish technology, our jewellery is designed to last longer, reducing the cycle of "throwaway" fast fashion.
  • Ethical Sourcing: We work closely with our artisan partners to ensure fair labor practices and safe working environments that honor traditional Indian craftsmanship.
  • Packaging: We are continuously striving to reduce single-use plastics in our shipping materials, moving towards recyclable and reusable premium jewelry boxes.
