Maintenance & Longevity

Jewellery Care Guide

Preserving the everlasting shine and intricate detail of your cherished LilAura pieces.

1. Anti-Tarnish Everyday Jewellery

Our anti-tarnish collection features high-grade stainless steel coated with durable 18K gold PVD (Physical Vapor Deposition), making them highly resistant to water, sweat, and everyday wear.

  • Water Friendly: You can comfortably wear these pieces while showering, swimming, or washing your hands.
  • Chemical Caution: While water-resistant, prolonged exposure to harsh chlorine, abrasive household cleaners, or concentrated perfumes can dull the gold luster over time. Apply lotions and perfumes before putting on your jewellery.
  • Cleaning: Gently wipe down with a soft microfibre cloth after wear to restore natural brilliance.

2. Traditional Heritage Brass Jewellery

Traditional Indian heritage pieces—such as Palakka necklaces, temple jewellery, and brass kadas—require gentle, mindful handling to protect their gold coatings and delicate stonework.

  • Keep Dry: Always remove your traditional brass pieces before showering, swimming, or engaging in strenuous physical activity. Moisture and perspiration can accelerate wear on traditional platings.
  • Perfumes & Cosmetics: Ensure perfumes, hairsprays, and skincare products are fully absorbed by your skin before wearing your heritage jewellery.
  • Post-Wear Care: Wipe clean with a dry, soft cotton cloth immediately after use to remove natural body oils before storing.

3. Storage & Safeguarding

Proper storage prevents scratching, tangling, and unnecessary oxidation when your pieces are not being worn.

  • Store individual pieces in separate soft pouches or lined jewellery boxes.
  • Keep your jewellery in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and humid environments (such as unventilated bathrooms).
