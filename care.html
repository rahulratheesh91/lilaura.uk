Jewellery Care Guide

To preserve the beauty of your LilAura pieces, proper maintenance depends entirely on the specific material composition of your jewellery.

Everyday Collection

18K PVD Stainless Steel

Crafted from robust 316L/high-grade stainless steel with advanced Physical Vapor Deposition (PVD) gold plating, our everyday cuffs, kadas, and minimalist necklaces are engineered for active lifestyles.

  • 100% Waterproof: Wear them in the shower, pool, or sea without fear of fading.
  • Sweat & Gym Safe: Highly resistant to perspiration and body oils.
  • Anti-Tarnish Guarantee: Maintains its brilliant golden luster over years of continuous wear.
  • Cleaning Tip: Simply wipe down with a soft microfiber cloth after wear to remove dust.

Heritage Collection

Gold-Coated Brass & Temple Sets

Forged in traditional brass and finished with a classic antique or matte gold coating, our South Indian Palakka, Meenakari, and Kasumala sets capture authentic cultural heritage.

  • Keep Away from Moisture: Store in a dry pouch; avoid wearing during swimming or bathing.
  • Perfume & Lotion Care: Apply cosmetics, hairsprays, or perfumes *before* putting on your heritage necklace set.
  • Gentle Storage: Keep pieces individually wrapped in soft fabric to protect delicate stones and enamel work.
  • No Harsh Chemicals: Never use abrasive jewellery cleaners or chemical dips on gold-coated brass.
