Jewellery Care Guide

LilAura carries two distinct collections. Please identify your piece below to ensure its eternal shine.

Anti-Tarnish Stainless Steel

18K PVD Gold Plated

Our modern collection is crafted from high-grade titanium stainless steel and coated using an advanced 18K Physical Vapor Deposition (PVD) process. This creates an incredibly durable, hypoallergenic shield.

  • Water & Sweat Proof: You can safely wear these pieces in the shower, at the gym, and in the ocean without fear of rusting or fading.
  • Skin Safe: Guaranteed never to turn your skin green.
  • Cleaning: Simply wipe down with a soft microfiber cloth occasionally to remove natural body oils and restore the brilliant gold shine.

Heritage Traditional Brass

Classic Gold Coating

Our authentic Indian heritage pieces (such as the Palakka and Meenakari chokers) are cast in traditional brass to honor centuries of artisan craftsmanship. They feature a beautiful, but delicate, standard gold coating.

  • Keep It Dry: Strict avoidance of water is required. Do not wear these pieces in the shower or while swimming.
  • Avoid Chemicals: Apply all perfumes, hairsprays, and lotions before putting on your heritage jewellery to prevent the coating from reacting.
  • Storage: When not in use, store your traditional brass pieces in the provided LilAura box or a dry, airtight pouch away from direct sunlight.
