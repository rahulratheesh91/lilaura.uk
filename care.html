Jewellery care

Care for your LilAura jewellery.

Care instructions depend on the product world and the individual product record.

HERITAGE

Traditional gold-coated brass

Keep Heritage pieces dry unless the individual product record explicitly states otherwise. Avoid perfume, hairspray, lotions and prolonged contact with water. Store in a dry pouch or jewellery box.

Important: Heritage is not automatically waterproof, water-resistant or anti-tarnish.

EVERYDAY

Stainless steel & 18K PVD

Follow the claim shown on your product page. Some Everyday records specify water-resistant or waterproof wear and anti-tarnish performance; not every product should inherit those claims.

Best practice: even where water claims exist, rinse and dry after exposure to chemicals, salt or chlorine where appropriate.
