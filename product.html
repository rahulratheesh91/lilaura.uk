While our stainless steel pieces are designed for 24/7 wear (including in the shower), we recommend keeping all traditional brass pieces dry to preserve their intricate hand-painted finishes. Gently wipe your jewelry with a soft cloth after wearing, and store it in a dry, cool place to maintain its eternal shine.
