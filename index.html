What is Palakka jewellery?

Palakka jewellery is a traditional South Indian jewellery style associated particularly with Kerala, recognised for its characteristic green stone motifs.

Is Heritage jewellery waterproof?

No blanket waterproof claim applies to the Heritage collection. Heritage products are traditional gold-coated brass unless the individual product record states otherwise.

Which jewellery is designed for everyday wear?

Our Everyday world contains stainless-steel products. Specific anti-tarnish, water-resistant or waterproof claims are shown only when supported by the individual catalogue record.
