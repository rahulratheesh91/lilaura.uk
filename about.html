The Origin

"Why must beautiful things be hidden away?"

We grew up watching the women in our lives treat their jewellery like fragile secrets. Beautiful Kundan sets were wrapped in cotton, locked in safe boxes, and only brought out for weddings. Daily wear was restricted to boring, simple pieces because "real gold is too risky" and "artificial jewellery fades in a week."

We believed you shouldn't have to choose between Tradition and Freedom.

Lilaura was born to break the glass case.

The Innovation

Not Just Gold Plating.
A Golden Shield.
