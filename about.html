Our story

Born in Britain. Inspired by India.

LilAura brings together two jewellery worlds: Indian heritage and modern everyday design.

A modern relationship with heritage.

LilAura was created around a simple idea: jewellery can carry the richness of cultural tradition while fitting naturally into contemporary life.

Our Heritage world draws from Indian jewellery traditions, including Kerala and South Indian-inspired styles. Current product records describe these pieces primarily as traditional gold-coated brass, with some records specifying brass/alloy.

Our Everyday world focuses on modern stainless-steel jewellery, including pieces described as 18K PVD gold plated. Wear-related claims are kept product-specific so customers can understand exactly what applies to the piece they are considering.

Clarity is part of luxury.

We believe premium presentation should be matched by accurate product information, thoughtful care guidance and an easy route to personal assistance.
